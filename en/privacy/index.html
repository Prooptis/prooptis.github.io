Privacy notice

Short and simple: what we do with the details you send us.

Who we are

The controller is Prooptis Single Member P.C. (GEMI no. 195398703000, tax ID 803365672), Greece.

What we collect

Only what you enter in the contact form: name, company, sector or product, email, phone (optional) and your message.

Why we use it

Only to reply to your request and, if you wish, arrange an intro call. The legal basis is your consent and steps you request before a possible engagement (Art. 6(1)(a) and (b) GDPR). We do not send newsletters or ads and we do not sell or share your details with third parties for their own purposes.

How it reaches us

The form sends your message by email to the company through the form delivery service Web3Forms, acting as a processor for delivery only. We may reply from a different company email address.

How long we keep it

As long as needed for our conversation. If we do not start working together, we delete the message within 12 months unless you ask us to keep it.

Your rights

You can ask for access, correction or deletion of your data, or withdraw consent, by replying to our email or through the contact form. You may also complain to the Hellenic Data Protection Authority (www.dpa.gr).

Cookies

This website uses no tracking or advertising cookies. Fonts are hosted on the site itself.
