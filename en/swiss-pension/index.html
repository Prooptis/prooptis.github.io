Worked in Switzerland? Let’s find what is yours.

We help with the paperwork and the language for your Swiss pension. In plain words, step by step.

Ask us, no commitment

Anyone who worked in Switzerland usually has rights in two schemes: the state pension (AHV/AVS) and the employer’s occupational pension (2nd pillar).

Many people who returned to Greece, or their children, do not know there is money there, or struggle with German and the forms. We take care of the administrative and language work on the file, with your written authorisation.

What we do

  • Searching for forgotten 2nd-pillar accounts through the Zentralstelle 2. Säule (Central Office 2nd Pillar)
  • Preparing and following up an application for a Swiss AHV/AVS pension
  • Collecting documents, translations and German-language correspondence with the funds
  • Plain-language updates at every step

Who it is for

People who worked in Switzerland and now live in Greece, and heirs who want to know whether there is money there.

To be clear

  • We do not give legal, tax or investment advice. Where needed, we refer you to a licensed professional.
  • We never handle your money. Every amount is paid by the fund directly into your own account.
  • We act only with your written authorisation.
  • You can also search with the Zentralstelle yourself. We take care of the language, the documents and the follow-up until the end.

FAQ

Can I do the search myself?

Yes. You can search with the Zentralstelle 2. Säule yourself. If you prefer, we take care of the language, the documents and the follow-up until the end.

Does the money pass through you?

No, never. Every amount is paid by the fund directly into your own account.

What do you need from me to start?

At first only what the form asks: your name, phone and where and roughly when you worked in Switzerland. File documents are needed later, only if we work together and with your written authorisation.

Can children or heirs ask?

Yes. If your parent worked in Switzerland, tell us what you know about where and when, and we will explain the next steps.
