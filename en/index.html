Why Switzerland

A market that rewards those who enter it the right way.

Switzerland is close, stable and values quality. For many Greek products and services it is a natural next market.

It is also demanding. Each canton has its own rules, languages change from region to region, and buyers work through referrals and trust. That is where we come in.

FAQ

What people usually ask.

Do I need a Swiss company to sell in Switzerland?

Not always. Many businesses start by exporting from Greece or through a distributor and set up a company later. In the needs assessment we look at what fits you, with input from the right professionals.

How does company formation in Switzerland work?

The steps depend on the legal form and the canton. They usually include articles of association, a bank account for the capital, a notary and registration in the commercial register. We coordinate the whole process with lawyers, accountants and banks.

How do you find Swiss customers for my product?

We map who buys products like yours (buyers, distributors, partners), approach them with the right message and use introductions from our network. The goal is real meetings, not mass emails.

Do you give legal or tax advice?

No. Licensed lawyers and accountants in Switzerland do, and we introduce you to them. We coordinate the work so it moves correctly and quickly.

Which sectors do you work with?

Any sector: food, industry, agricultural machinery, software, services and more. What matters is a good product or service and a real wish to enter the Swiss market.

Do you help with investors or fundraising?

No. We focus on establishment in Switzerland and commercial development, meaning finding customers and partners.

How long does it take?

It depends on the legal form, the canton, the bank and your sector. The plan gives you a realistic timeline, without sugar-coating.

How much does it cost?

The intro call is free. After that you get a written proposal for your project, with third-party costs (e.g. notary, register) shown separately.

Do you guarantee customers?

No, and no serious partner would. We guarantee methodical work, the right introductions and honest updates on what is moving and what isn't.
